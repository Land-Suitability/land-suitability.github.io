The core of the system: turn buyer demand, suitability, water and climate into a seasonal production plan.

Key questions

  • Which crop and variety should each cluster plant, on how many hectares, and when?
  • Which plan maximises farmer net income within land, water and demand limits?
  • What rotation and intercropping keep soils healthy?

Scope and components

  • Matching crops to soil, water and agroecology
  • Variety and maturity-period evaluation
  • Expected yield and profitability comparison
  • Seasonal production plan optimisation (market-backwards)
  • Rotation and intercropping strategy

Data sources

  • Stage 1 to 3 outputs
  • Stage 5 demand signals
  • Variety trial data

Outputs

  • Seasonal Production Plan (cluster x crop x variety x hectares x planting date)

Indicators (KPIs)

  • Planned versus realised net income
  • Share of contracted demand covered by the plan
