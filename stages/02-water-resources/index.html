02. Water Resource Assessment

How much water is actually available for each cluster, in each season, and at what sustainable rate?

Key questions

  • What surface and groundwater can each cluster use sustainably?
  • How does seasonal water demand compare with supply?
  • Where would irrigation investment unlock the most suitable land?

Scope and components

  • Surface water: rivers, lakes, reservoirs, streams
  • Groundwater availability and sustainable extraction (requires hydrogeological field data)
  • Watershed and catchment assessment
  • Existing irrigation infrastructure and accessibility
  • Water demand versus supply balance

Data sources

  • Sentinel-1 / Sentinel-2 water masks
  • CHIRPS rainfall
  • Hydrological models
  • Borehole and geophysical surveys

Outputs

  • Cluster x season water budgets (m3)
  • Irrigation opportunity map

Indicators (KPIs)

  • Water productivity (kg per m3)
  • Share of plans within the sustainable water budget
