Key questions

  • What will buyers demand at harvest time, and at what price?
  • Which buyers can sign offtake agreements before planting?
  • Which competing production areas affect prices?

Scope and components

  • Domestic and regional demand assessment
  • Wholesalers, retailers, processors and exporters
  • Historical and current price analysis
  • Harvest-time demand and price forecasting
  • Pre-season offtake agreements

Data sources

  • Market price surveys
  • ECX data where relevant
  • Buyer interviews
  • Trade statistics

Outputs

  • Demand signals (crop, tonnes, grade, delivery window, price)
  • Offtake contracts

Indicators (KPIs)

  • Share of produce sold through planned agreements
  • Price forecast error
