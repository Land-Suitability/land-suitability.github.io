Key questions

  • Which plots are S1, S2, S3 or N for each candidate crop and season?
  • What are the soil, slope and erosion constraints on each plot?
  • How are smallholder plots grouped into planning clusters, given land-use certificates?

Scope and components

  • Land availability and land-use certificate (tenure) records
  • Soil properties: fertility, pH, texture, drainage, depth
  • Topography: elevation, slope, aspect, erosion risk
  • Satellite land-cover and land-use analysis
  • Land suitability classification (FAO framework, MCDA/AHP, ML/DL)

Data sources

  • Sentinel-2
  • Landsat
  • SRTM / Copernicus DEM
  • SoilGrids / EthioSIS
  • Field soil samples

Outputs

  • Plot x crop suitability scores with uncertainty
  • Constraint maps
  • Planning clusters

Indicators (KPIs)

  • Suitability model accuracy against field trials
  • Share of pilot plots mapped and classified
