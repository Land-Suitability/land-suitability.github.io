Key questions

  • When will Meher and Belg rains start and stop this year?
  • What is the drought, flood and frost risk per cluster?
  • How long is the effective growing season?

Scope and components

  • Historical weather analysis
  • Rainfall onset and cessation prediction
  • Temperature, humidity and evapotranspiration
  • Drought, flood and frost risk
  • Growing-season length; Meher and Belg planting windows

Data sources

  • CHIRPS
  • ERA5
  • Local weather stations
  • Seasonal forecasts

Outputs

  • Seasonal climate outlook per cluster
  • Planting windows
  • Risk scenarios

Indicators (KPIs)

  • Onset forecast error (days)
  • Lead time of drought alerts
