Amhara Integrated Agriculture EcosystemConcept brief · 1 / 2

From land intelligence
to market delivery

An integrated agricultural system for the Amhara Region of Ethiopia that connects land, water, climate, crops, farmers, markets, storage, transport and customers into one coordinated value chain. Plan backwards from the market to the land; execute forwards from land preparation to the final customer; learn from every season.

The problem and the approach

Today: push

  1. Grow what was grown last year
  2. Inputs arrive when they arrive
  3. Everyone harvests and sells at once; prices drop
  4. Transport is ad hoc; losses are not measured
  5. Nobody learns what worked

With the ecosystem: pull

  1. Start from what buyers will take
  2. Match it to land, monthly water and climate risk
  3. Order inputs backwards from planting day
  4. Monitor, harvest, store and move on schedule
  5. Settle fairly; feed the lessons back

Pilot roadmap (18 to 24 months)

Months 1 to 4Establish the pilot
  • Select one woreda
  • Register farmers, map plots
  • Assess land, soil, water
Months 3 to 8Production and market
  • 1 to 2 crops from demand
  • Secure inputs
  • Buyer agreements before planting
Months 6 to 14Deploy the platform
  • Geospatial database
  • Suitability and water maps
  • Advisory and monitoring
Months 12 to 24Measure and expand
  • Income, yield, losses
  • Compare with non-pilot kebeles
  • Expand on evidence

Potential partners

Regional and woreda agriculture offices and water authorities; regional universities and agricultural research institutes; national agricultural transformation and research institutes; multilateral food and agriculture organisations and rural development funds; seed and fertilizer suppliers, transporters, warehouses, processors and exporters; farmer cooperatives, unions and water-user associations. These are potential partners to approach; no partnership is implied.
